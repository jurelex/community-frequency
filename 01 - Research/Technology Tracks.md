# Technology Tracks

Community Frequency evaluates technologies as tools within a broader resilient communications architecture.

## Meshtastic

Meshtastic is being evaluated as one low-power mesh technology, including field testing with Heltec V4 hardware.

## MeshCore

MeshCore is being evaluated as a separate low-power mesh technology, including infrastructure and companion-node testing.

## Comparison

The objective is not to select a technology by preference, but to understand where each approach performs well, where it does not, and how either could complement HF, satellite, Internet, cellular, or other systems.

Detailed operational configuration and sensitive network information remain in the Master Vault.
