# Methodology

Community Frequency follows an evidence-driven field approach:

1. **Listen** — understand existing communications systems, community needs, constraints, and local knowledge.
2. **Measure** — conduct controlled tests and document observable results.
3. **Learn** — compare results, identify limitations, and revise assumptions.
4. **Build** — only after evidence supports an appropriate architecture.

The project does not assume that one technology is the answer.
