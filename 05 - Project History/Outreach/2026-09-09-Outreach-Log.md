## 6. Campaign Rules / Positioning

1. Community Frequency is presented as an **independent research project**, not as a nonprofit or 501(c)(3).
2. The research question begins with the communications needs of rural and geographically isolated communities in Peru.
3. Mesh is the technology being evaluated; **Meshtastic and MeshCore are current technical tracks**, not predetermined solutions.
4. Do not lead manufacturer outreach with a fixed equipment wish list.
5. Ask manufacturers for recommendations on appropriate platforms and configurations for comparative testing.
6. Ask distributors and Peruvian suppliers about the actual acquisition, replacement, maintenance, and support costs in Peru.
7. Where appropriate, explore a **technology partner / ecosystem partner / supply partner** relationship rather than assuming a formal partnership.
8. Public documentation may acknowledge meaningful support, equipment, guidance, or collaboration where appropriate; do not promise endorsement, guaranteed visibility, or formal partnership status before an actual relationship exists.
9. Future outreach should be sent from **`renato@communityfrequency.org`**.
