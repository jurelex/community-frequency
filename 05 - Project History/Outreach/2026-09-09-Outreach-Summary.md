# Community Frequency Outreach Summary — September 2026

**Purpose:** Public project-history record of the September 2026 outreach campaign.

**Project:** Community Frequency  
**Research focus:** Whether affordable, low-power mesh communications can help connect rural and geographically isolated communities in Peru.

> This summary intentionally omits personal contact information, direct email addresses, ticket numbers, private correspondence, and other internal campaign details.

## Outreach completed

### Academic / research collaboration

Eight academic/research contacts in Peru were contacted to explore:

- research methodology and technical review;
- relevant telecommunications, wireless, LoRa, IoT, and rural-communications expertise; and
- potential future student or research participation.

### Manufacturer / technology / distribution outreach

Eleven organizations were contacted across the mesh/LoRa ecosystem, including:

- hardware manufacturers;
- RF and specialized mesh hardware developers;
- field hardware and accessory providers;
- U.S. and international distributors;
- Peruvian technology distributors; and
- mesh ecosystem organizations.

The outreach focused on research collaboration, comparative hardware evaluation, technical guidance, procurement and supply-chain information, and potential technology/ecosystem collaboration.

Two additional organizations were contacted through official web contact forms.

## Outreach approach

The campaign presents Community Frequency as an **independent research project** focused on rural and geographically isolated communities in Peru.

The research question comes first. Mesh communications are the technology being evaluated, with **Meshtastic and MeshCore** as current technical tracks.

Organizations were not approached with a predetermined equipment list. Depending on the organization, the outreach explored:

- evaluation or loaner hardware;
- research or quantity pricing;
- technical guidance;
- hardware and configuration recommendations;
- procurement, availability, replacement, and maintenance realities in Peru; and
- potential technology, ecosystem, or supply-chain collaboration.

## Response tracking

Substantive responses and private contact details are maintained separately from this public project-history record.

Where an organization provides meaningful support, equipment, technical guidance, or establishes a formal relationship with Community Frequency, that relationship may be documented publicly in an appropriate project section.

## Status as of September 9, 2026

The initial outreach round is complete. The next phase is to monitor responses, conduct follow-ups where appropriate, and incorporate useful information into the research and field-testing process.
