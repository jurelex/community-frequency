# Peru 2026 Assessment

## Purpose

The Peru mission is a feasibility and discovery assessment for resilient communications in remote and underserved communities.

The project is not approaching the mission with a predetermined technology solution.

## Questions

- What communications systems already exist?
- Where are the actual connectivity gaps?
- How are HF, satellite, cellular, Internet, radio, and other systems being used?
- What are the community's operational needs?
- What environmental and geographic constraints affect low-power communications?
- What regulatory requirements apply?
- What maintenance, training, ownership, and sustainability models are realistic?

## Planned mission

The current plan begins September 27, 2026.

Findings will be documented as observations rather than assumptions, with conclusions updated as evidence becomes available.
